 ## English

# 🤡 LinkedShit

Tired of the hollow, "inspiring," and "strategic" nonsense on LinkedIn? Had enough of the "humbled and honored" crowd or those embarking on a "new journey" every single day? 

**LinkedShit** is here for exactly that. This extension detects the fake sincerity and corporate jargon (bullshit) in your LinkedIn feed, calls it out, and sends those meaningless emojis back where they belong: the circus (🤡).

## ⚡ What Does It Do?

- **Jargon Hunter:** Instantly catches empty buzzwords like "synergy," "ecosystem," and "paradigm shift" and marks them in red.
- **Emoji Purge:** Replaces all that fake smiling and success-themed emoji clutter with 🤡.
- **Custom Dictionary:** Got some specific words that give you an allergic reaction? Add them yourself, and the filter kicks in immediately.
- **Privacy:** We don't care about your data. Everything starts in your browser and stays in your browser.

## 🛠 Installation (Stop Talking, Start Installing)

1. **Grab the Files:** Click the **Code** button and select **Download ZIP**. Extract it to a folder—just don't delete that folder, or the extension will break.
2. **Developer Mode:** Go to `chrome://extensions` in your browser and toggle **Developer Mode** on the top right.
3. **Load It Up:** Click **Load unpacked** and select the folder you just extracted.
